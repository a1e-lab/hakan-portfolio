## Our Approach

In every project, we strive for balance between **idea**, **function**, and **form**. At Hakan Karaman Architecture, we believe spaces should be not only aesthetically pleasing but also livable, sustainable, and in harmony with their environment.

## Services

- **Residential** — Living spaces where modern and traditional design coexist harmoniously
- **Commercial Buildings** — Functional commercial spaces that reflect brand identity
- **Public Structures** — Civic spaces that serve the community
- **Cultural Heritage** — Contemporary interpretations while preserving historical values

## Design Process

1. **Needs Analysis** — Identifying project requirements and user needs
2. **Concept Design** — Idea development and spatial organization
3. **Detailed Design** — Comprehensive drawings and technical specifications
4. **Construction Support** — Site supervision and project management
